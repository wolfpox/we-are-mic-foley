Record sounds with your mouth, hands, desk or a towel. Shape them with plain effects, stack them in layers, make a set of variations and export a game-ready pack. Your recording stays the star.

01

Record your sounds

Tip: make the same sound 3 to 5 times in one recording, with a short pause between. Each one becomes a take, and every variation uses a different take. That's what makes them sound natural.

Shape it

Everything starts off, so you hear your recording as it is. Pitch also changes length, like a tape: lower is slower and bigger, higher is quicker and smaller. 12 semitones (st) is an octave.

Stack sounds: a slap plus a low thump makes a bigger hit.

03

Variations and export

Games repeat sounds constantly. Each variation uses a different take and a different pitch, so the 40th footstep doesn't sound like the first.

Files are named like sound_01.ogg.

You've changed the sound since these were made. Make variations again to update them.
